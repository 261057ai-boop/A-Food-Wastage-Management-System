About Our Project

Food Wastage Management System is a platform that helps reduce food waste by connecting food donors with needy people and charitable organizations. Hotels, restaurants, event organizers and individuals can donate excess food instead of throwing it away.

How It Works

01. Donate

Register and provide details about your excess food.

02. Connect

Food details are shared with people and organizations.

03. Distribute

Collected food is safely distributed to needy people.
